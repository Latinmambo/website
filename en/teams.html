Ready to take your dancing to the next level?

Join an amazing community of dancers led by Lenin & Génesis.

We train together, grow together, and travel across Europe to perform at renowned salsa festivals. From vibrant stages to unforgettable backstage moments, being part of our teams means living the full artist experience.

If you dream of improving your technique, performing on international stages, or simply connecting with passionate dancers, you can also be part of this experience.

We have 4 official teams:

  • 🔥 Latin Mambo Student-Team
  • ⚡ Latin Mambo Team
  • 🌸 Génesis Solorzano Ladies Team
  • 💪 Men Style Team

If you have questions or special needs, we're here to help! We look forward to meeting you.

JOIN AND LIVE THE EXPERIENCE WITH US!

🔥 LATIN MAMBO STUDENT-TEAM

Ideal for intermediate dancers or those with little experience who want to grow step by step in a motivating and fun environment.

What we offer:

  • ⭐ On2 technique, musicality and strong fundamentals.
  • ⭐ Fun rehearsals in a relaxed environment.
  • ⭐ A real community starting the same journey as you.
  • ⭐ First performances in small shows.
  • ⭐ Confidence and progressive growth.

⚡ LATIN MAMBO TEAM

The main team, designed for experienced dancers who want to be part of professional shows on international stages.

What we offer:

  • 🔥 Advanced training and professional technique.
  • 🔥 High-level choreographies.
  • 🔥 Performances at European festivals.
  • 🔥 Professional networking.
  • 🔥 Discipline and excellence.

🌸 GÉNESIS SOLORZANO LADIES TEAM

A space created especially for women who want to work on elegance, feminine technique, and stage presence.

What we offer:

  • 🌺 Feminine technique: arm styling, spins, posture, and musicality.
  • 🌺 Powerful and expressive choreographies.
  • 🌺 Supportive and motivating environment.
  • 🌺 Personal and artistic growth.
  • 🌺 Performances at events.

💪 MEN STYLE TEAM

For men who want to develop a strong, clean, and masculine style both on the social dance floor and on stage.

What we offer:

  • 🔥 Masculine On2 style: power, posture, and control.
  • 🔥 Dynamic and energetic choreographies.
  • 🔥 Personal stage identity.
  • 🔥 Local and international performances.
  • 🔥 A male community that pushes you to improve.
