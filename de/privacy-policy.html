Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. Introduction

At Latin Mambo, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you contact us or use our website.

2. Data We Collect

When you contact us through our website form, we collect the following information:

  • Name – to personalize our communication with you.
  • Email address – to respond to your inquiries and send relevant information.
  • Phone number – optional, to contact you more quickly if necessary.
  • Message content – the text you write in the contact form.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data will be used only for the purpose of responding to your inquiries, providing information about our classes or events, and maintaining communication related to Latin Mambo activities.

4. Legal Basis

The processing of your data is based on your consent (Article 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at info@latinmambo.com.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Your information is stored securely and only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. However, we use trusted external services (e.g., Formspree) to process contact form submissions securely.

7. Your Rights

According to the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your stored personal data
  • Request correction of incorrect information
  • Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise your rights, please contact us via email at info@latinmambo.com.
